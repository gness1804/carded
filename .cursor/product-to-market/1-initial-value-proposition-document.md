# Initial Value Proposition Document

## Working directory

`~/Desktop/carded`

## Contents

Below is the contents of what another AI agent told me about the value proposition of bringing this carded application to market. 


Why Most Promising:
•⁠  ⁠Clear, universal pain point - Everyone hates manually entering business card data
•⁠  ⁠Simple value proposition - "Take photo → Get contact" is instantly understandable
•⁠  ⁠Privacy-first differentiator - BYOK (bring-your-own-key) model appeals to security-conscious professionals
•⁠  ⁠Existing market validation - Business card apps exist (CamCard, etc.) but privacy concerns are real
•⁠  ⁠Low barrier to entry - Simple use case, quick user adoption
•⁠  ⁠B2B potential - Sales teams, recruiters, conference attendees all need this

To Make Market-Viable:
•⁠  ⁠Add bulk processing - Scan multiple cards in one session (conference mode)
•⁠  ⁠CRM integrations - Export directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, LinkedIn
•⁠  ⁠Team features - Shared contact databases for sales teams
•⁠  ⁠Mobile-first rebuild - Make it a native iOS/Android app (not just web)
•⁠  ⁠Freemium model - Free for X scans/month, paid for unlimited + team features
•⁠  ⁠OCR fallback - Handle cards Claude's vision can't parse well
•⁠  ⁠Analytics - Track when contacts were added, from which events

Monetization:
•⁠  ⁠$4.99/month individual (unlimited scans)
•⁠  ⁠$19.99/month team (5 users, shared contacts)
•⁠  ⁠Enterprise pricing for large sales orgs

Start with Carded (#1) because:
•⁠  ⁠Easiest to validate (test with local networking groups)
•⁠  ⁠Clearest ROI for users (time saved)
•⁠  ⁠Lower risk than advice column
•⁠  ⁠Can build in weeks, not months
•⁠  ⁠Privacy-first angle is a real differentiator

Revenue potential: 1,000 paid users at $4.99/month = $60K/year. 50 enterprise teams at $100/month = additional $60K/year. This is achievable within 12-18 months with focused marketing to sales/recruiting communities.
